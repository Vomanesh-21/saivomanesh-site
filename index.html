TikTok Ads Platforms | Performance Marketing & Client Solutions

Overview

Worked across TikTok advertising platforms with SMB and key client accounts across industries including telecom, financial services, automotive, food, clothing, and technology. Focused on campaign performance, advertiser growth, product adoption, measurement, and client-facing platform strategy.

Problem

Advertisers faced tracking, campaign setup, account access, billing, and platform configuration challenges that impacted campaign delivery, measurement accuracy, and optimization. They also needed stronger campaign execution, clearer performance insights, scalable spending strategies, and better use of TikTok Ads solutions to improve conversions, ROAS, and long-term account growth.

Approach

Reviewed campaign setup, targeting, bidding, budget allocation, conversion tracking, Pixel and Events Manager signals, account structure, and performance metrics. Translated insights into optimization recommendations, product-fit opportunities, account growth strategies, and reporting that aligned with advertiser goals.

Impact

Helped advertisers improve campaign clarity, scale performance more effectively, strengthen measurement confidence, and adopt relevant TikTok Ads solutions. Contributed to better client outcomes while identifying opportunities for retention, upsell, cross-sell, and platform revenue growth.

E-commerce Customer Segmentation

Overview

Used customer purchase behavior to segment users and improve marketing targeting.

Problem

Customers were being treated the same despite different buying behavior, value, and retention potential.

Approach

Impact

Leukemia Detection Using Artificial Intelligence

Overview

Problem

Approach

Tools

Python, Machine Learning, Random Forest, Tkinter, healthcare analytics.
